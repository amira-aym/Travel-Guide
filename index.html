Travel Tips

Always carry versatile clothing layers and a universal adapter. Roll your clothes to save space and prevent wrinkles.

Knowing how to say "Hello", "Thank you", and "Please" in the local language goes a long way in making a good impression.

Scan your passport, ID, and travel insurance. Email them to yourself and keep an offline copy on your phone.

Even in highly digital countries, small shops, street vendors, or public transport might only accept cash. Keep some local money just in case.

Dress appropriately according to local traditions, especially when visiting religious or conservative areas, to show respect for the culture.
