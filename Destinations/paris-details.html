Paris, France

Known as the City of Light, Paris is celebrated for its world-class art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture. Stroll along the Seine, marvel at iconic monuments, and immerse yourself in quintessential Parisian life.

What To Experience

Eiffel Tower & Champ de Mars

Take in panoramic views of Paris from the summit or enjoy a picnic on the lawns below.

Louvre Museum

Home to the Mona Lisa and thousands of masterworks spanning centuries of human history.

Café de Flore & Saint-Germain

Relax at historic sidewalk cafés and savor world-renowned French pastries and espresso.
